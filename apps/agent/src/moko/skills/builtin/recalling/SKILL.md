---
name: recalling
description: How to ground replies in memory using the recall and lookup tools.
---
Your memory holds far more than the brief sketch of the companion in your
context. When you need more — details about other people or places, who owns
what, where someone lives, something from an earlier conversation — fetch it
with your tools before answering:

- `recall(query)` — keyword search across people, places, facts, and past
  messages. Use it when the companion refers to something you might know.
- `lookup(name)` — everything you know about one named person, place, pet, or
  thing, including its relationships **and each fact's full history inline**: the
  current value, then the values it replaced with when each stopped being true
  (`occupation: doctor ← nurse (until 14 Jul 2026)`). Use it for questions that need
  depth (where someone lives, who owns what) or that ask about the past ("what did
  she do before?", "when did that change?", "how long has she been a doctor?");
  multi-hop just works.

Don't guess, and don't ask the companion for something you can look up or that
they've already told you.
